Shera Studio guide

Frequently asked questions.

Find clear answers about classes, massage, booking, payments, and how to make the most of your time with Shera.

01Do I need experience to join a class?

No. Our classes are suitable for beginners as well as experienced participants. Sherazade provides guidance and modifications based on your fitness level and goals.

02How do I register?

If you're a new client, simply create your client profile and choose the class package that is right for you before you begin booking online. You can contact Shera by WhatsApp, text message, or email for any help needed.

03Where is Shera Studio located?

265 Finsbury Avenue, Stittsville, Ottawa, Ontario.

04Is parking available?

Yes. Free street parking is available near the studio. Please ensure your vehicle is parked legally and in accordance with all local parking regulations.

05Do you offer online classes?

Yes. Selected Pilates and stretching sessions are available through Zoom for clients worldwide.

06What should I bring to class?

Wear comfortable workout clothing and bring a water bottle and a small towel. For everyone's comfort, please arrive in clean workout attire and maintain good personal hygiene, as classes involve close instruction and shared equipment.

07What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothing that allows free movement is recommended.

08How long are the classes?

Most classes are approximately 60 minutes.

09Are private sessions available?

Yes. Private Pilates sessions are available for clients who prefer one-on-one instruction or have specific rehabilitation or fitness goals.

10Can I switch between group and private sessions?

Yes, depending on your package and availability. Contact Shera to discuss the best option.

11Do you issue insurance receipts?

Yes. Receipts are provided for eligible extended health insurance claims.

12Which massage is right for me?

Shera can recommend the most appropriate treatment based on your goals, whether you're seeking pain relief, recovery, stress reduction, or relaxation.

13How long are massage appointments?

Most massage appointments are 60 minutes.

14What should I expect during my first massage?

Shera will discuss your health history, concerns, and treatment goals before beginning your session.

15How do I book a class?

Once your client profile and package are set up, you can book available classes through the online booking calendar.

16Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes. You can cancel or reschedule your booking according to the studio's cancellation policy. Contact Shera if you need assistance.

17What happens if a class is full?

You won't be able to reserve a spot once the class reaches capacity. Contact Shera about alternative sessions.

18What if I don't have any sessions remaining?

Contact Shera to purchase or renew your class package before booking additional classes.

19Will I receive a confirmation after booking?

Yes. A confirmation email will be sent after your booking is completed.

20Can I add my booking to my calendar?

Yes. You can add your booking to Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook.

21What payment methods do you accept?

Please contact Shera for current payment options.

22Do you offer class packages?

Yes. Contact Shera to learn about available class packages and pricing.

23Can I attend if I have an injury?

In many cases, yes. Exercises can be modified to suit your condition. Please inform Shera before your session.

24Are classes suitable for seniors?

Yes. Sessions can be adapted to different ages, mobility levels, and fitness abilities.

25Can pregnant or postpartum clients attend?

Yes. Shera has training in postpartum rehabilitation and pelvic floor recovery. Please contact her before booking.

26What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations or missed appointments may be subject to the studio's policy.

27What happens if Shera needs to reschedule a class?

All registered clients will be notified by email and offered the updated class time or an alternative session.

28How can I contact Shera?

You can reach Shera by WhatsApp, text message, email, or the website contact form.

29When should I arrive, and what should I do after class?

To provide the best experience for every client, please arrive 5 minutes before your scheduled class so sessions can begin promptly and without interruption.

At the end of your class, please:

  • Sanitize your mat and any equipment you used using the cleaning supplies provided.
  • Leave the studio within 5 minutes after the class ends.

This allows Shera enough time to prepare the studio, sanitize shared spaces, and welcome clients for the next session. Thank you for helping us maintain a clean, organized, and respectful environment for everyone.

30Why should I choose Shera Studio?

Shera Studio offers far more than traditional Pilates, stretching, and massage. Every session is personally designed and led by Sherazade Mami, whose extensive background in kinesiology, rhythmic gymnastics, therapeutic massage, and over 15 years of movement experience allows her to create a truly customized experience for every client.

Rather than following a fixed routine, Sherazade combines the most effective techniques from Pilates, stretching, mobility training, rehabilitation principles, and therapeutic bodywork to develop a program tailored specifically to your body's needs. She carefully evaluates your posture, movement patterns, flexibility, strength, and muscular imbalances to target areas that need the most attention.

Whether your goal is to relieve pain, improve posture, recover from an injury, build core strength, increase flexibility, enhance athletic performance, or simply move with greater confidence, every session is adapted to help you achieve lasting results safely and effectively.

At Shera Studio, you're not just joining a class; you will enjoy expert guidance, individualized attention, and a holistic approach designed to help your body move better, feel stronger, and perform at its best.
