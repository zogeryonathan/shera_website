How it works

Four easy steps

  1. 1

    Create your profile

    Enter your first name, last name, and email below. Your profile begins with zero sessions.

  2. 2

    Choose a package with Shera

    Message Shera to choose your class package. Shera adds the sessions to your profile.

  3. 3

    Verify your email

    When you reserve a class, enter your details and we email a six-digit verification code. It protects your account for 30 minutes.

  4. 4

    Book your session

    Choose your class and attendance type. Your available balance updates immediately after each confirmed booking.

Your class balance

Always know what you have left.

For example, if Shera adds a package of 10 sessions, you see 10 sessions available. Book one class and the balance becomes 9. If you cancel more than 24 hours before the class, one session is returned to your balance.

24-hour cancellation policy: online cancellation is available only when your class begins more than 24 hours later. For late changes, please contact Shera directly.

Keep Shera Studio close

Install Shera’s website as an app

Android

  1. Open the Shera Studio website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

iPhone

  1. Open the Shera Studio website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
